Dear colleagues,

Every day, more than 31 nationalities work side by side at AMP-2. Today we're turning that diversity into connection.

We're launching Language Champion, a peer-to-peer learning program where colleagues teach colleagues: 100 words, 10 sessions, one language at a time. To make it happen, we're looking for 10 Language Champions, two for each of our five languages, to lead the sessions and share their culture with the team.

APPLY HERE: [INSERT REGISTRATION LINK]
Deadline: Monday 12 October, end of shift

OUR FIVE LANGUAGES
- Arabic (marhaba)
- Turkish (merhaba)
- French (bonjour)
- Urdu (khush amadeed)
- Spanish (hola)

WHO SHOULD APPLY
- You speak one of the five languages natively or fluently
- You enjoy helping others learn, and you are patient and encouraging
- You can explain things clearly and simply
- You can commit to all 10 sessions (November to December), plus orientation on Wednesday 14 October and a planning workshop on Sunday 18 October
No teaching experience is needed. Our L&D team will train you and give you a ready-made session kit.

WHAT YOU'LL GAIN
- Skill development: facilitation and coaching training, plus real experience leading a group
- Recognition: an official Champion pin, a certificate and leadership appreciation at our December celebration
- Networking: new connections across every shop, department and nationality, and visibility with site leadership

HOW SELECTION WORKS
1. Apply through the link above by Monday 12 October.
2. A committee from HR, L&D and site leadership scores every application against clear, published criteria: language proficiency, teaching and mentoring willingness, communication skills, and availability.
3. Shortlisted applicants are invited to a friendly 10-minute conversation on Tuesday 13 October.
4. Everyone hears back by the end of Tuesday 13 October.
If you aren't selected this round, we'll invite you to be a Native Buddy, helping learners practice between sessions.
